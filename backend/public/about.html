About HARVION & The Adventist Faith

"Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." — Revelation 14:12

🌟 What is HARVION?

HARVION is an enterprise-grade digital ministry and literature evangelism platform engineered to empower the Seventh-day Adventist remnant church worldwide in fulfilling the Great Commission (Matthew 28:18–20) and heralding the Three Angels' Messages (Revelation 14:6–12).

In an era dominated by superficial distractions, HARVION unites the foundational pillars of historic Adventism into a seamless modern digital sanctuary: The Infallible Word of God (KJV Bible), The Spirit of Prophecy (Ellen G. White Writings), Medical Missionary Health Reform (The Right Arm of the Gospel), Literature Evangelism (Colporteur Ministry), Pillars of the Adventist Home, and Practical Country Living & Organic Agriculture.

"In a special sense Seventh-day Adventists have been set in the world as watchmen and light-bearers. To them has been entrusted the last warning for a perishing world. On them is shining wonderful light from the Word of God. They have been given a work of the most solemn import—the proclamation of the first, second, and third angels' messages. There is no other work of so great importance. They are to allow nothing else to absorb their attention." — Ellen G. White, Testimonies for the Church, Vol. 9, p. 19

✝ How HARVION Matches the Adventist Faith

Every module, algorithm, database schema, and interface in HARVION has been deliberately crafted in strict adherence to the 28 Fundamental Beliefs of the Seventh-day Adventist Church:

✓ 1. The Word of God

The Holy Scriptures are the supreme, authoritative, and infallible revelation of God's will. HARVION includes a complete, offline-first King James Version (KJV) reader with book, chapter, and verse cross-referencing.

✓ 2. The Three Angels' Messages

Revelation 14:6–12 is the heart of HARVION's evangelism: heralding the everlasting gospel, the judgment hour, true worship of the Creator, the fall of spiritual Babylon, and keeping the commandments of God and the faith of Jesus.

✓ 3. The Spirit of Prophecy

In accordance with Revelation 12:17 and 19:10, the testimony of Jesus is the Spirit of Prophecy. HARVION hosts 50 curated Ellen G. White masterworks with both full offline chapters and verified direct links to the official EGW Writings platform.

✓ 4. The Seventh-Day Sabbath

The Sabbath (Genesis 2:1–3; Exodus 20:8–11) is honored as the perpetual memorial of creation and seal of God. Sabbath School quarterly lessons, study notes, and PDF exports are integrated directly into the system.

✓ 5. Christ in the Sanctuary

Present truth emphasizes Christ's high-priestly ministry in the Most Holy Place of the heavenly sanctuary and His imminent, personal, audible, and visible return in glory (Titus 2:13).

✓ 6. The Temple of the Holy Ghost

1 Corinthians 6:19–20 dictates that the body is God's temple. Medical missionary work is the enter-in wedge. HARVION educates believers on the 8 natural laws of health (NEWSTART) and pure nutrition.

🏛 The Five Operational Pillars of HARVION

  • 📚
    I. Literature Evangelism & Colporteur Ministry: Ellen G. White declared that publications must go everywhere "like the leaves of autumn." HARVION provides registered colporteurs with personal portal pages, digital tracts sharing, and live camp meeting location mapping so seekers can easily find and purchase physical and digital truth-filled literature.
  • 🌿
    II. Medical Missionary Work (The Right Arm of the Gospel): Integrating *The Ministry of Healing*, *Counsels on Diet and Foods*, and *Counsels on Health*. Educating families on hydrotherapy, herbal poultices, plant-based recipes, charcoal remedies, and lifestyle disease reversal without commercial drugs.
  • 👨‍👩‍👦
    III. Pillars of the Adventist Home: Dedicated portals addressing courtship, sacred marriage, parenting, and family worship based on *The Adventist Home* and *Child Guidance*. Helping youth maintain purity in thought and conduct amidst modern moral decay.
  • 🌾
    IV. Country Living & Agricultural Self-Reliance: Faithful to the divine counsel to move out of large cities into rural settings. HARVION provides organic farming handbooks, soil conditioning guides, raised bed designs, and agro-forestry techniques to foster family self-reliance and build character.
  • 🎥
    V. Digital Soul-Winning & Livestream Broadcasting: Reaching contemporary generations where they are—including TikTok LIVE and YouTube integrations—under strictly moderated Adventist codes of conduct, language transparency, and automatic broadcast notifications to believers.
