Unshakable Foundation

The Six Pillars of Present Truth

HARVION stands firmly upon the historic landmarks of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, established through diligent Bible study under the guidance of the Holy Spirit.

1. Sola Scriptura

The Holy Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments are the inspired Word of God, the authoritative revealer of doctrines, and the unerring standard of character.

2 Timothy 3:16-17 • Isaiah 8:20

2. The Spirit of Prophecy

One of the gifts of the Holy Spirit is prophecy. This gift is an identifying mark of the remnant church and was manifested in the ministry and writings of Ellen G. White.

Revelation 12:17 • Revelation 19:10

3. The Seventh-Day Sabbath

The beneficent Creator, after the six days of Creation, rested on the seventh day and instituted the Sabbath for all people as a memorial of Creation and a sign of sanctification.

Genesis 2:1-3 • Exodus 20:8-11

4. Christ's Heavenly Ministry

There is a sanctuary in heaven, the true tabernacle which the Lord set up. In 1844, at the end of the 2300 prophetic days, Christ entered the Most Holy Place to begin the investigative judgment.

Hebrews 8:1-2 • Daniel 8:14

5. The Three Angels' Messages

The proclamation of the Everlasting Gospel, the call to worship the Creator, the warning against Babylon's fall, and the mark of the beast, preparing a people for the Second Advent.

Revelation 14:6-12

6. Health & Christian Temperance

Our bodies are the temples of the Holy Spirit. We advocate a wholesome lifestyle, vegetarianism, the 8 laws of health (NEWSTART), and total abstinence from harmful substances.

1 Corinthians 6:19-20 • 3 John 1:2
