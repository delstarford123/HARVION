Privacy & Trust

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Published by DELSTARFORDWORKS for the HARVION Ministry Platform.

1. Introduction

Welcome to HARVION, developed and operated by DELSTARFORDWORKS. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and providing a consecrated, distraction-free environment for spiritual worship, sacred hymnody, and literature evangelism.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and web ecosystem collect, use, and protect your information in full compliance with global privacy regulations and Google Play Store Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

HARVION is engineered as an offline-first platform. You can read the 66 Books of the King James Bible, all 50 Spirit of Prophecy books, and sing along to the 695 SDA Hymnal completely offline without creating an account or transmitting personal information.

We only process information when you voluntarily use specific interactive ministry features:

  • Literature Bookstore & Hard Copy Orders: When purchasing missionary physical books via M-Pesa, we collect your name, phone number, and delivery SDA Book Center/church outpost to process payments and deliver parcels.
  • Live Worship & Ministry Broadcasting: When a registered pastor, evangelist, or church media officer starts a livestream, the broadcast title, speaker name, and stream URL (YouTube or TikTok embed) are published to the ministry feed.
  • Customer Support & Prayer Requests: When submitting a prayer request or support inquiry, your email or WhatsApp contact is used solely to respond spiritually to your request.

3. Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information. Period.

Any interaction data is protected using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption in transit. We partner only with reputable, privacy-certified infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud Firestore and Authentication for secure realtime synchronization of public church announcements and orders.
  • Safaricom Daraja M-Pesa: Compliant, bank-grade mobile payment processing for East African book orders.

4. Device Permissions

The HARVION Android mobile application requests only the essential permissions needed for sacred function:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream authentic piano audio tracks, receive live worship notifications, and verify connectivity status.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Allows uninterrupted, continuous hymn piano playback and text-to-speech audio during church services and family devotionals.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. In the mobile app's Notification Center, you can delete and dismiss notifications at any time.

If you wish to have all order records or ministry accounts permanently removed from our databases, you may email us at harvion@delstarfordworks.co.ke with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." All associated data will be deleted within 48 business hours.
