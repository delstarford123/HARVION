Unshakable Foundations

How HARVION Aligns with Biblical Truth

HARVION was created specifically to safeguard, proclaim, and distribute the historic landmarks and distinctive truths entrusted to Seventh-day Adventists.

1. The Bible Only (Sola Scriptura)

The Holy Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments are the written Word of God, given by divine inspiration. The Bible is the authoritative standard for character, test of experience, and unerring revealer of doctrines.

2 Timothy 3:16-17 • Isaiah 8:20

2. The Spirit of Prophecy

One of the gifts of the Holy Spirit is prophecy. This gift is an identifying mark of the remnant church and was manifested in the life and ministry of Ellen G. White. Her writings are a continuing source of comfort, guidance, and correction.

Revelation 12:17 • Revelation 19:10

3. The Seventh-day Sabbath

The gracious Creator, after the six days of Creation, rested on the seventh day and instituted the Sabbath for all people as a perpetual memorial of His creative work. It is the fourth commandment and the eternal sign of God's authority.

Genesis 2:1-3 • Exodus 20:8-11

4. The Heavenly Sanctuary & 1844

There is a sanctuary in heaven, the true tabernacle which the Lord pitched. In 1844, at the end of the prophetic 2300 days of Daniel 8:14, Jesus entered the Most Holy Place to commence the Investigative Judgment and cleansing of the sanctuary.

Daniel 8:14 • Hebrews 8:1-2

5. The Three Angels' Messages

The final proclamation of the Everlasting Gospel in Revelation 14:6–12 warns the world against the beast and his image, calls humanity back to worship the Creator, and reveals the patience of the saints who keep God's commandments.

Revelation 14:6-12

6. Health Reform & Temperance

Our bodies are temples of the Holy Ghost. Total abstinence from all intoxicating and harmful substances, paired with the eight natural remedies (NEWSTART) and a wholesome vegetarian diet, prepares mind and body for spiritual discernment.

1 Corinthians 6:19-20 • 3 John 1:2
Our Vision & Origin

Why HARVION Was Built for This Hour

In an age overwhelmed by digital noise, social media distraction, and theological compromise, believers and gospel workers needed a consecrated, fast, and completely distraction-free platform.

Many believers in rural areas, camp meetings, and mission fields struggle with weak internet connections or expensive data packages. HARVION was engineered from the ground up as an offline-first ecosystem, storing all 50 Spirit of Prophecy books and daily lessons directly on the user's phone.

Furthermore, Literature Evangelists (Colporteurs) who sacrifice their lives walking from town to town required modern tools: real-time camp meeting broadcasting, referral lead tracking, and automated mobile money payments via M-Pesa.
